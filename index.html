ABOUT ANFAL ENTERPRISES

Reliable FMCG Distribution. Built on Trust.

ANFAL ENTERPRISES was established to bridge consumer demand with seamless wholesale fulfillment across Bhatkal and the coastal belt of Karnataka. Situated strategically on National Highway 66 (N.H. 66) in Nawayath Colony, our facility serves as a vital logistics node connecting national FMCG conglomerates with local retail stores, regional supermarkets, and institutional merchants.

Under the leadership of Mr. Imtiyaz Hussain, our company upholds strict benchmarks for product authenticity, temperature-controlled inventory preservation, and rapid order turnaround. We ensure that our retail clients benefit from stable supply, wholesale rate transparency, and verified branded goods with full GST compliance.

✓
High-Capacity Warehousing: Spacious depot equipped for secure bulk storage and systematic FIFO rotation.
✓
Strategic Highway Connectivity: Direct N.H. 66 location enabling fast dispatch and regional vehicle accessibility.
✓
100% Genuine Manufacturer Sourcing: Zero counterfeit risk with verifiable batches directly from FMCG principals.
✓
Transparent B2B Billing: Detailed GST invoices, clear payment terms, and volume rebate structures.
